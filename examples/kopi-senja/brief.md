# Kopi Senja × Studio Rantai — 1-Page Brief
**Stay for the Golden Hour**  ·  October 2026  ·  Prepared for Kopi Senja

## The Big Idea — Golden Hour Club
*The best part of the day starts when work ends.*
We reposition Kopi Senja from a commuter pit-stop to the city's after-work ritual. Every outlet becomes a 'golden hour' spot: warm light, a dusk-only menu, and creators who make 5pm the moment you look forward to.
> “Clock out, glow up — see you at Senja.”

## How We Tackle It — Tease → Launch → Sustain
1. **Own a time slot** — Every asset says 4–7pm. We sell a moment, not a menu.
2. **Creators as regulars** — Micro creators who live near outlets post repeatedly, not once — repetition builds the ritual.
3. **Make it physical** — Light, playlist and stamp card make the idea visible in-store, so content and reality match.

## Service Mix

- **Creative Strategy & Social** — One idea across every channel.
